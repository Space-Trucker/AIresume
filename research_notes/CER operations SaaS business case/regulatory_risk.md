Method note: nearly every primary site (gse.it, mase.gov.it, arera.it, eur-lex, agenziaentrate.gov.it) and most trade press (pv magazine Italia, QualEnergia, Rinnovabili.it, Staffetta, Edilportale, cerinrete.it, teleborsa, ènostra) returned EGRESS_BLOCKED on fetch. Where I could not confirm a date or figure, it is listed under Gaps. "Enacted" means published law, decree or regulator decision. ## 1. Legal basis and dated regulatory timeline (what is enacted law vs. proposal)

### Takeaway
The CER/CACER regime is enacted law at every level. The layers are: D.Lgs. 199/2021 (RED II transposition, amended by L. 60/2025 and by D.Lgs. 5/2026 transposing RED III); DM MASE 414 of 7 Dec 2023, amended by DM 127 of 16 May 2025; ARERA TIAD 727/2022/R/eel, amended by 15/2024; GSE Regole Operative, approved by MASE directorial decrees of 23 Feb 2024, 22 Apr 2024 and 17 Jul 2025, plus the separate Facility CACER rules of 27 Mar 2026; the EU state-aid approval of Nov 2023; and DL 19/2026 (the "DL PNRR", converted by L. 50/2026), which turned the PNRR grant into a "facility" with concession agreements due by 30 Jun 2026 (+60 days) and commissioning by 31 Dec 2027. The regime has been amended roughly every 6–9 months since 2024.

### Inferences
- The rulebook changed at least 8 times in 30 months: DM 414, DD 22, DD 170, L. 60/2025, DM 127, DD 229, DL 19/2026 and the 27 Mar 2026 Facility rules, plus portal changes in Dec 2025 and Apr 2026. For a SaaS vendor this is recurring compliance-maintenance cost, and it is also a moat: small CERs cannot track these changes themselves.
- Everything that governs the installed base (the 20-year tariff, TIAD valuation, the 55%/45% allocation rule) is enacted and has an EU state-aid decision behind it. The open risks sit at the edges: new entries after 2027, the PNRR grant timeline, and administrative throughput.

### Gaps
- Exact GU publication and entry-into-force dates for DM 414/2023 and ARERA 727/2022 could not be fetched from primary sources (egress blocked). DM 414 is commonly reported in force from 24 Jan 2024, but I did not verify this here.
- I could not confirm whether GSE has formally used the 60-day extension, or the exact last concession-agreement date. "~29 Aug 2026" is arithmetic, not a sourced date.
- I did not verify the brief's label "6th PNRR revision" for the Nov 2025 change. Sources only say "PNRR revision" plus the Council decision of 27 Nov 2025.

## 2. Tariff horizon: 5 GW cap / 31 Dec 2027 end; successor scheme; RED III and EMD energy-sharing transposition

### Takeaway
Under enacted law, access to the 20-year CACER premium tariff closes on 31 Dec 2027, or earlier if 5 GW is reached. The EU state-aid approval covers the non-RRF part only until 31 Dec 2027. As of Sept 2026 no successor scheme or extension has been enacted or notified to the Commission. There are only stakeholder calls (e.g., Legacoop). Italy transposed RED III (D.Lgs. 5/2026) and the EMD 2024/1711 energy-sharing right (D.Lgs. 3/2026), and was not among the 18 states targeted for energy-sharing non-transposition on 25 Sep 2026. ARERA's implementing rules (proceeding 223/2026) are still pending, and I found no evidence that the new energy-sharing route carries an incentive.

### Inferences
- **For the SaaS business, the tariff cliff is about new logos, not the installed base.** Every configuration admitted by 31 Dec 2027 keeps a 20-year tariff, so the operating base (and admin demand) runs to about 2045–2047. With no successor scheme, the rate of new CER formation should drop sharply in 2028.
- The 5 GW cap looks very unlikely to bind before 31 Dec 2027. About 300 MW is operational and 1.76 GW is financed under the PNRR (Section 3), so the date, not the capacity, is the binding constraint. There is a real "use it or lose it" rush in 2027: commissioning-deadline pressure is a sales window.
- Italy transposed Art. 15a before the deadline, so the energy-sharing right (non-CER, possibly bilateral sharing within a market zone) becomes a new legal route once ARERA finishes 223/2026. A market-zone perimeter is far wider than the primary-substation (cabina primaria) perimeter CERs use today. It may create a new, possibly unincentivised, "energy sharing" product category. That could either add new customer types for a sharing-settlement platform or let large players bypass the CER model. Its economic value depends on ARERA's network-charge and valuation rules, which have not been published.
- Any post-2027 incentive would likely need a new state-aid notification (the 2023 approval ends 31 Dec 2027), which usually takes months. If nothing is notified in 2026–H1 2027, a gap in 2028 is likely.

### Gaps
- I found no GSE public-counter figure for how much of the 5 GW tariff allocation has been requested or used (the GSE site was blocked).
- I found no MASE statement, draft decree, or budget-law (Legge di Bilancio 2027) proposal on a post-2027 CER scheme. The Legacoop statement is undated in the snippet.
- Whether D.Lgs. 3/2026 energy sharing carries any incentive or network-charge benefit (like TIAD valuation) could not be checked. ARERA's final rules are pending.

## 3. PNRR cut (€2.2bn → €795.5m), MASE justification, unfunded projects, rescue measures through 2026

### Takeaway
MASE cut the PNRR CER grant to €795.5m on 21 Nov 2025, when requests reached €1.456bn (about 3.3 GW). In Aug 2026 it added €246m, bringing the facility to €1,041.5m, of which about €1.014bn was granted to 38,390 beneficiaries. That leaves roughly €0.4bn of eligible demand unfunded (my estimate). As of mid-Sept 2026, 1.76 GW was financed but only about 300 MW was operating. The real risk has moved from "funding" to "commissioning by 31 Dec 2027". Rescue so far: the €246m top-up, the DL PNRR facility with longer timelines, and regional FESR calls (e.g., Marche, Jul 2026).

### Cited Findings
- MASE (21 Nov 2025) explained that the original €2.2bn was based on 2021 simulations assuming zero-interest loans covering 100% of costs. That design was later replaced by grants of up to 40% under state-aid rules, which need far less money. MASE also framed the cut as aligning resources with actual project progress and tight PNRR deadlines. — [MASE](https://www.mase.gov.it/portale/-/pnrr-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili-aggiornata-la-dotazione-finanziaria-a-795-5-milioni); [InfobuildEnergia](https://www.infobuildenergia.it/pnrr-dotazione-cer-scende-cosa-cambia-imprese-territori/)

- Projects deemed eligible but not funded "remain valid for future funding if additional resources are provided". — [Valori.it / MASE](https://valori.it/comunita-energetiche-fondi-pnrr/)
- When the window closed on 30 Nov 2025, requests totalled €1.456bn against €795.5m available. — [Edilportale Sep 2026 via search](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/cer-e-autoconsumo-246-milioni-in-piu-dal-pnrr_111683_27.html)

### Inferences
- Unfunded demand is about €1.456bn − €1.0415bn ≈ €0.41bn, consistent with the brief's "~€400m". This is arithmetic, not an official figure. Since RRF granting had to close by 31 Aug 2026, no further PNRR money can rescue these projects. Any rescue must come from national, regional or cohesion funds.
- The funding-to-operation gap (1.76 GW financed vs about 300 MW on) with a hard 31 Dec 2027 commissioning date means several hundred MW to over 1 GW may miss the deadline and lose the 40% grant. Some projects may still commission without the grant (the tariff alone at the 55% threshold) if the tariff window is open.
- For the SaaS business, the 38,390 beneficiaries (many are individual applicants or members, not distinct CERs) and the 3,630 PNRR configurations in operation form a large near-term pool of configurations that must do PNRR reporting (rendicontazione) plus 20 years of tariff administration.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the legal vehicle for the Aug 2026 €246m (MASE decree, PNRR reallocation, or national funds).

- Other regional calls (Lombardia, Emilia-Romagna, Sardegna, Puglia, etc.) were not catalogued.

## 4. Operational rules that create recurring admin work (payments, member changes, statutes, reporting, tax, GDPR)

### Takeaway
Running a CER involves recurring, rule-bound work:
- monthly GSE advance payments plus an annual true-up (conguaglio) that needs member-level allocation
- continuous member and POD changes through the GSE SPC portal (up to 90-day reviews, no silent approval)
- statute clauses enforcing the 55%/45% "excess tariff" rule
- PNRR reporting (rendicontazione)
- GSE fees (DM 106/2024)
- tax treatment that depends on the CER's legal form (ETS/non-commercial vs company)
- GDPR controller duties over member metering data

Rules on all of these changed in 2024, 2025 and 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Payments / settlement**
- GSE pays monthly advances based on estimated incentivisable shared energy and the applicable tariff, then recognises the actual incentive from the year after the reference year, using meter data the network operators send to GSE. — [GSE Regole Operative / GSE "Corrispettivi e tariffa" via search](https://www.gse.it/servizi-per-te/autoconsumo/gruppi-di-autoconsumatori-e-comunita-di-energia-rinnovabile/corrispettivi-e-tariffa)
- Shared-energy valuation is monthly once GSE has complete valid meter data. Values are published by the 25th of month m+1. The true-up (conguaglio) is paid from the following year (by 15 May, per the snippet). Payments go out monthly once minimum thresholds (€50 / €100 in the snippet) are reached. — [GSE Regole Operative (Allegato 1), via search](https://www.gse.it/documenti_site/Documenti%20GSE/Servizi%20per%20te/AUTOCONSUMO/Gruppi%20di%20autoconsumatori%20e%20comunita%20di%20energia%20rinnovabile/Regole%20e%20procedure/ALLEGATO%201%20Regole%20Operative%20CACER.pdf) (thresholds as reported; exact wording not verified)
- GSE charges fees to beneficiaries under the Decreto Corrispettivi (DM 106 of 15 Mar 2024). — [Rinnovabili.it](https://www.rinnovabili.it/energia/comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili/cacer-pubblicato-il-decreto-corrispettivi-per-il-gse/)
- The Jul 2025 rules allow credit assignment and collection mandates (a CER can mandate a third party to collect the incentive), provided the excess-tariff destination rule is respected. — [Mygreenbuildings](https://www.mygreenbuildings.org/2025/07/18/nuove-regole-operative-gse-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili.html)

**Statute / allocation rule**
- The share of the tariff above 55% of shared energy (45% when combined with the PNRR grant) must go only to non-business consumers and/or social purposes in the territory. This must be written into the CER statute (or the private agreement for self-consumer groups). The Regole Operative give non-exhaustive examples of "social purposes". — [AIMAG/GSE slides](https://www.aimag.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/240409_AIMAG_GSE.pdf); [Strategie Amministrative](https://www.strategieamministrative.it/dettaglio-news/20246211217-il-decreto-cacer-in-breve-la-tariffa-incentivante/)
- Membership rules (L. 60/2025): associations, ATER, IPAB, public-service companies and reclamation consortia may join. Members may be outside the plant's municipality, but control stays with eligible subjects located where the plants are. — [Enti Locali Online](https://www.entilocali-online.it/convertito-in-legge-n-60-2025-il-decreto-bollette-che-introduce-alcune-importanti-novita-per-le-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili/)

**Member entry/exit and POD changes**
- GSE added SPC-portal functions to add or remove PODs and/or production plants in already-qualified configurations. Automatic checks cover ownership, same primary substation and existing Scambio sul Posto contracts. This followed the removal of "numerous registry burdens" in Dec 2025, with the portal intended as the single point for managing a community. — [pv magazine Italia 28 Apr 2026](https://www.pv-magazine.it/2026/04/28/cer-gse-ora-e-piu-semplice-modificare-le-configurazioni-gia-approvate/); [Nextville](https://www.nextville.it/news/63602/cer-e-autoconsumo-nuove-funzionalita-sul-portale-gse); [Ambiens 17 May 2026](https://www.ambiens.org/2026/05/17/cer-e-autoconsumo-nuove-funzioni-gse-e-la-voce-delle-criticita-di-cisambiente/)
- Members must promptly tell the CER about changes (residence, POD code, plant decommissioning, etc.). — [Community-CER](https://www.community-cer.it/configurazioni-cer-come-aggiungere-membri-e-aggiornare-lo-statuto/)
- The SPC application in the GSE client area is where the configuration's referent requests the shared-energy service and PNRR contributions. — [GSE CER page via search](https://www.gse.it/servizi-per-te/autoconsumo/gruppi-di-autoconsumatori-e-comunita-di-energia-rinnovabile/comunit%C3%A0-energetiche-rinnovabili)
- Review times follow the Regole Operative: a 90-day target, with no silent approval. GSE concludes payment-request reviews within 60 days, net of certain procedures. — [QualEnergia, Sep 2026](https://www.qualenergia.it/pro/articoli-pro/gse-richieste-cer-decuplicate-ma-coda-sara-smaltita/)

**PNRR reporting**
- GSE issued rendicontazione instructions for M2C2 I1.2 on 2 Feb 2026. The Facility rules of 27 Mar 2026 govern concession agreements and commissioning within 24 months, by 31 Dec 2027 at the latest. — [GSE PDF](https://www.gse.it/documenti_site/Documenti%20GSE/Servizi%20per%20te/AUTOCONSUMO/Gruppi%20di%20autoconsumatori%20e%20comunita%20di%20energia%20rinnovabile/Regole%20e%20procedure/Istruzioni%20operative%20rendicontazione%20CER%20020226.pdf); [MyEnergy](https://www.myenergy.it/blog/incentivi-e-agevolazioni/regole-operative-facility-cacer-2026/)

**Tax**
- AdE Risoluzione n. 18/E of 12 Mar 2021: for individuals acting outside a business, the tariff premium is not taxable income and restituted tariff components are not tax-relevant, but the price paid for selling energy is. For a CER that is a non-commercial entity, only energy-sale revenue is subject to IRES. For VAT, the tariff premium and restituted components are outside scope, as non-repayable grants. — [FiscoOggi](https://www.fiscooggi.it/portale/-/comunit%C3%A0-energetiche-rinnovabili-il-punto-fra-normativa-e-prassi); [Fiscalità dell'Energia](https://www.fiscalitadellenergia.it/2022/02/24/il-trattamento-fiscale-delle-somme-corrisposte-dal-gestore-della-rete-alle-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili-e-agli-autoconsumatori-collettivi/); [Eutekne](https://www.eutekne.info/Sezioni/Art_912543_comunita_energetiche_con_tariffa_premio_incentivante_fuori_campo.aspx)
- AdE Risposta a interpello n. 22 of 9 Feb 2026: a CER that is a Third-Sector Entity (ETS) registered in RUNTS may withhold part of the GSE incentives to cover its higher institutional management costs. Those amounts are neither commercial income nor subject to VAT, provided the CER does not offer members additional services. Reasons given:
  - no synallagma (no specific service in exchange)
  - economically equivalent to a top-up of the annual membership fee
  - producing and sharing renewable energy is an activity "of general interest" for ETS
  — [MySolution 10 Feb 2026](https://www.mysolution.it/fisco/informazioni/news/2026/02/10/cer-ed-ets-le-trattenute-sugli-incentivi-gse-non-sono-attivita-commerciale/); [FiscoOggi](https://www.fiscooggi.it/portale/-/comunit%C3%A0-energetiche-rinnovabili-le-trattenute-sono-fuori-campo-iva); [Redazione Fiscale](https://www.redazionefiscale.it/c/fisco-passo-passo/49336-trattenute-sugli-incentivi-gse-e-natura-non-commerciale-il-chiarimento-dellagenzia-delle-entrate)
- **Discrepancy with the brief:** the brief cites a "March 2026 ruling" on trattenute. The ruling found is Risposta n. 22 of **9 Feb 2026**. Commentary appeared through 27 Feb 2026 ([Segretari Comunali](https://www.segretaricomunalivighenzi.it/comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili-le-trattenute-sono-fuori-campo-iva/27/02/2026/)). I found no separate March 2026 ruling.

**GDPR**
- Practitioner guidance says the CER legal entity is the data controller, either sole or joint depending on the model. Typical legal bases are contract performance, legal obligation (mandatory communications to GSE) and legitimate interest. Data handled includes identity data, smart-meter consumption and production data, incentive amounts and derived profiles. A DPIA is "often necessary", along with Art. 32 security measures. — [Il Sole 24 Ore NT+ Condominio](https://ntpluscondominio.ilsole24ore.com/art/i-mercoledi-privacy-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili-cer-e-rispetto-gdpr-AGHFoVZ); [Osservatorio CER (snippet only)](https://www.osservatoriocer.it/privacy-comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili/)

### Inferences
- The recurring work suited to software is:
  - monthly reconciliation of GSE advances
  - the annual conguaglio and member-level redistribution, honouring the 55%/45% rule and any statutory withholdings
  - managing member and POD changes through the SPC portal
  - PNRR reporting through 2027
  - fee tracking
  - tax classification by legal form (ETS vs cooperative vs company)
  - GDPR records and DPIA
  Every item has changed at least once since 2024, which supports a subscription model over one-off setup.
- Risposta 22/2026 favours CERs set up as RUNTS-registered ETS that fund operations, including SaaS fees, by withholding from incentives. But an interpello binds only the applicant, and the "no additional services to members" condition limits bundling of value-added services. Product design should keep member-facing extra services separate from the institutional withholding.
- The Jul 2025 credit-assignment and collection mandates open a way for third parties (such as a platform or its payment partner) to collect and distribute incentives. That adds payment-flow features, and also AML/payments-licensing questions for the SaaS.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the exact monthly-advance mechanics, the €50/€100 thresholds, or the conguaglio date. The GSE PDF was blocked and the snippet may mix up rules.
- I found no AdE guidance on the 4% withholding (ritenuta) on GSE payments to companies or commercial CERs, or on IRES/IRAP for CERs set up as companies or cooperatives.
- I found no Garante Privacy decision or guideline specific to CERs, and no GSE statement on its own GDPR role for SPC or metering data.
- I did not verify whether GSE requires annual self-declarations or periodic reporting beyond PNRR rendicontazione for tariff-only configurations.

## 5. Data access: GSE portal vs APIs; DSO (e-distribuzione) data; SII / Portale Consumi; Chain2

### Takeaway
I found no evidence of a public GSE API for CER operators. GSE access runs through the Area Clienti SPC portal and an Open Data portal for aggregate data. Member-level metering reaches GSE from DSOs under TIAD, and operators see it only through the portal. The main new channel is ARERA's Portale Consumi (run by Acquirente Unico on the SII). Since 1 Oct 2025 end customers can authorise qualified third parties, including TIAD configurations such as CERs, to access their consumption data. e-distribuzione offers 15-minute load curves in its customer web portal. Chain2 gives near-real-time, local-only data from 2G meters to in-home devices. D.Lgs. 5/2026 promises better data access and interoperability for CERs, but implementing detail has not been verified.

### Cited Findings
- GSE runs an Open Data portal with free access to data on incentives it manages, in open formats. — [GSE Open Data](https://www.gse.it/dati-e-scenari/open-data)
- CER referents request the shared-energy service and PNRR grants through the SPC application in the GSE Area Clienti. The CER "Vetrina" is also in the Area Clienti. GSE provides an interactive map of primary substations. — [GSE CER page via search](https://www.gse.it/servizi-per-te/autoconsumo/gruppi-di-autoconsumatori-e-comunita-di-energia-rinnovabile/comunit%C3%A0-energetiche-rinnovabili); [GSE Vetrina](https://www.gse.it/servizi-per-te/autoconsumo/vetrina-delle-cer)
- A search for GSE download APIs or developer endpoints found none. Results covered only portal services. — [search summary](https://www.infobuildenergia.it/cer-online-portali-gse-per-gli-incentivi/)
- Shared-energy valuation relies on "energy measurements transmitted by network operators to GSE". — [GSE Corrispettivi e tariffa via search](https://www.gse.it/servizi-per-te/autoconsumo/gruppi-di-autoconsumatori-e-comunita-di-energia-rinnovabile/corrispettivi-e-tariffa)
- Portale Consumi (consumienergia.it, SPID login) is run by Acquirente Unico, which manages the SII national data hub, under ARERA's instructions. It shows up to 36 months of history, down to hourly electricity data. **Since 1 Oct 2025**, customers can authorise qualified third parties, explicitly including "configurations governed by TIAD (such as CERs)", ESCOs, suppliers and associations. — [Portale Consumi – Accesso di Terze Parti](https://www.consumienergia.it/portaleConsumi/it/accesso-di-terze-parti.page); [tuoingegnere summary](https://tuoingegnere.altervista.org/portale-consumi-arera/)
- Commentators have suggested programmatic (REST API) access to Portale Consumi, which implies it did not exist as a public API at the time. — [LinkedIn commentary via search](https://it.linkedin.com/pulse/il-portale-consumi-di-arera-dalla-consapevolezza-del-cliente-de-min) (weak source)
- e-distribuzione shows quarter-hourly, daily and monthly load curves online for customers with hourly or Open Meter (2G) meters. Producers can see readings and curves after registering. 2G meters record 96 quarter-hour values per day of active and reactive energy, both withdrawn and injected. — [e-distribuzione Curve di carico](https://www.e-distribuzione.it/servizi/contatore/curve-di-carico.html); [e-distribuzione Letture produttori](https://www.e-distribuzione.it/servizi/contatore/letture-produttori.html); [Open Meter guide](https://www.e-distribuzione.it/content/dam/e-distribuzione/documenti/open-meter/nuove-guide-open-meter/Una_Guida_su_cui_contare_Vademecum.pdf)
- Chain2 is a PLC channel (CEI-standardised, CENELEC C-band) from 2G "Open Meter" smart meters to user devices (DU). It delivers consumption data every few seconds, locally and free, "without intermediation of third parties", under ARERA-backed rules. Commercial DUs (e.g., Chain2Gate) target CERs. — [luce-gas.it](https://luce-gas.it/guida/contatore/smart-meter-chain-2); [Infoimpianti](https://www.infoimpianti.it/chain-2-servizi-post-contatore-nei-misuratori-elettrici-2g/); [Edyna](https://www.edyna.net/clienti/smart-meter/chain-2.html)
- D.Lgs. 5/2026 (RED III) includes measures on the availability and accessibility of data on renewable energy fed into the grid, "with particular attention to self-consumers and renewable energy communities", in Chapter III, which amends D.Lgs. 93/2011. — [Lavorofacile](https://www.lavorofacile.it/news/attuazione-direttiva-red-iii--il-d-lgs--5-2026-ridisegna-il-quadro-delle-fonti-rinnovabili-in-italia); [TuttoAmbiente](https://www.tuttoambiente.it/news/energie-rinnovabili-recepita-la-direttiva-red-iii/)

### Inferences
- A CER-ops SaaS today must combine three data paths: (a) GSE portal exports for settlement values, (b) member-authorised metering from Portale Consumi or DSO portals, and (c) optional Chain2 hardware for real-time data. No path is a stable API, so there is ongoing integration risk (layout or login changes, scraping fragility). Portale Consumi third-party delegation, since Oct 2025, is the most scalable legal route to member-level data.
- The data provisions in RED III (D.Lgs. 5/2026) and the ARERA 223/2026 proceeding could produce API-like access. Watch both as possible upside, or as possible competition from a regulator-run tool.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether GSE offers a web service or B2B integration for large CER aggregators, or whether Portale Consumi delegation includes any machine-readable or bulk export.
- I found no ARERA rule requiring DSOs to give CER referents direct quarter-hour data for all members. The TIAD flow goes DSO → GSE, and I found no evidence it reaches the CER directly.
- Chain2 rollout share and any 2026 ARERA changes to 2G meter data rules (e.g., 575/2025 references to "direttive 2G") were not verified.

## 6. Ongoing disputes and state-aid position (Sept 2026)

### Takeaway
In Sept 2026 the main disputes were administrative, not legal. ènostra and the national CER network say GSE's PNRR workload (over 48,000 requests) has stalled tariff-only CERs, some with integrations unanswered for over 15 months. They demand a separate queue and guaranteed response times. GSE says requests rose about 10x in three months and promises answers within a month. Consumerismo sent GSE a formal notice (diffida) over eligible-but-unfunded PNRR projects and is weighing legal action. The state-aid basis (EU approval of 22 Nov 2023) covers the regime only to 31 Dec 2027.

### Cited Findings
- ènostra (15 Sep 2026): thousands of CERs are "in administrative limbo". As of Aug 2026 there were 6,161 active CER configurations averaging 10 members, and ènostra estimates real participation would be 3–5x higher if pending files were processed. The bottleneck is GSE resources absorbed by more than 48,000 PNRR requests, leaving tariff-only configurations in the queue. Demands: separate PNRR and tariff-only handling, fast processing of integrations pending since 2025, maximum response times and case tracking. — [pv magazine Italia 15 Sep 2026](http://www.pv-magazine.it/2026/09/15/enostra-migliaia-di-comunita-energetiche-bloccate-dai-ritardi-del-gse/); [YouBuild](https://youbuildweb.it/cer-migliaia-di-comunita-energetiche-bloccate-enostra-chiede-al-gse-tempi-certi-e-pratiche-separate-dal-pnrr/); [Staffetta Quotidiana](https://www.staffettaonline.com/articolo.aspx?id=407267)

### Inferences
- The GSE backlog is the most immediate operational risk. It slows new admissions and member additions, which is what drives per-member or per-POD SaaS revenue. It also makes SaaS features that track GSE cases and prepare complete, first-time-right submissions more valuable.
- The PNRR grant (RRF window to 31 Dec 2025) was re-scoped by the DL PNRR facility to concession by 30 Jun 2026 (+60 days) and commissioning by 31 Dec 2027. Any change to the tariff end date or budget beyond the €3.5bn likely needs a Commission amendment or new decision. That is a procedural gate and a source of delay.

## 7. Risk register for a SaaS business dependent on CERs existing and operating for their 20-year life

### Takeaway
The installed base is protected. Every configuration admitted by 31 Dec 2027 keeps a 20-year tariff backed by enacted law and an EU state-aid decision. The biggest risks are to growth: (1) the tariff window closes on 31 Dec 2027 with no successor scheme; (2) PNRR-financed plants miss commissioning; (3) GSE throughput delays new members and payments. Retroactive tariff cuts are low-probability but high-impact, and Italy has precedent for cutting existing incentives. Regulatory churn and weak data access are high-probability, moderate-impact, and partly an opportunity.
