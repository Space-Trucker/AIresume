# T3: Routes outside pure air (the grey zone) and why they were not chosen

T1 shows that light has to be *made at the image point*. In pure air only plasma can do that (T2). If the owner's R3 rule (no gas, fog or screen) were relaxed to allow *matter supplied and recovered by the projector*, three more routes open.

| Route | What it gives | Why not chosen (numbers) |
|---|---|---|
| **Acoustic bead swarm** (MATD-style: 1–2 mm beads levitated by ultrasound, lit by RGB spotlights) | Full colour, ~0.4–1 mW of light per bead, silent in the audio band, no chemistry | E9: film-scale strokes need 30–450 fast beads and 25–590 W of 40 kHz ultrasound. That gives **123–142 dB** in the room (public limit 100 dB, IRPA 1984). Even a 1–5 bead accent layer gives 114–126 dB. Beads are visible when unlit and knocked away by a touching hand. Viable only as a small, enclosed desktop toy. |
| **Photophoretic optical traps** (BYU optical trap display) | Full colour, µm detail | ~24–500 mW of 405 nm light per trapped particle. Hundreds of particles means tens to hundreds of watts of violet laser light in the room, a Class 4 eye hazard. Proven only at ~1 cm³ with one particle. |
| **Gated luminescent nanoparticle aerosol cell** (Sonnet idea round, F1: upconverting nanoparticles lit only where two IR beams cross) | Any colour, cinematic brightness at 0.3–10 W, silent, no NOx | It is an aerosol (a medium, R3). 30 nm particles do not settle and need HEPA-class recovery. Inhalation toxicology is unknown. Abandons "nothing but air". |
| **Near-eye helmet visor** (the owner's helmet idea) | Exact film quality for the wearer (full colour, any brightness) | It is eyewear (R2). But it is a proven, cheap, separate product line (visor HUD via waveguide or birdbath optics) and needs no plasma. |
| **Light-field room** (emitting walls, floor and ceiling) | Full colour, bright | Screens (R3/R4). The touch lemma (T1 §3) also erases content behind hands. |

**Conclusion.** Every route that reaches film colour and brightness does so by adding matter or eyewear. Within the owner's constraints the plasma engine is the only option. The grey-zone routes are recorded so a future owner decision ("particles are acceptable if contained") can reopen them. The bead swarm then needs a quieter (≥ 100 kHz) acoustic design, and the nanoparticle cell needs toxicology first.
